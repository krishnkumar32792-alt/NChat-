NChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

About NChat

NChat is a social communication application.

Information We Use

NChat may use information you provide in the app, such as your username, profile information, posts, comments and account information.

Camera and Photos

NChat may request camera and photo-library access for features such as taking photos and selecting profile or post images.

Nearby Feature

Nearby discovery is optional. NChat does not display your exact location to other users. Connection requests require acceptance before chatting.

Data Storage

Some current app data is stored locally on your device. If online services are added, applicable data handling will be described here.

Data Deletion

You can delete your NChat account from the Delete Account option inside the app. You may also use the account-deletion page provided by NChat.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the NChat developer through the contact information provided on the official NChat listing or website.
