Delete NChat Account

You can delete your NChat account from the NChat app using Settings → Delete Account.

Deletion Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting the NChat developer through the contact information provided on the official NChat listing or website.

What Happens After Deletion

Account-related information and locally stored NChat data associated with the account are removed according to the application's current data-handling process.

Some information may need to be retained where required by applicable law.
